- **Description:**

Know what's for dinner, every night this week.

Table Compass plans your family's meals, writes the shopping list, and remembers the little things, like pulling the chicken out of the freezer the night before.

PLAN THE WEEK IN A MINUTE
• Breakfast, lunch, dinner and dessert. Plan only the meals you want.
• Busy nights get quick or slow-cooker meals.
• Lots of variety, with no two taco nights in one week.
• Swap any meal with one tap.
• Want it simple? Choose "Just plan my meals" and hide the extras.

ALLERGIES AND DIETS
• Set allergies once and it never plans a meal that contains them.
• Vegetarian, vegan, keto, low sodium and more.

YOUR RECIPES, YOUR PHOTOS
• Over 80 family-friendly recipes to start.
• Add your own by typing or snapping a recipe card.
• Snap a photo of a dish you made and it shows on that meal everywhere.

A SHOPPING LIST THAT WRITES ITSELF
• Built from the week's meals and sorted by aisle.
• See what you paid last time and set a weekly budget.

DINNER ON TIME
• "Start cooking by 5:10." It counts back from your dinner time.
• Reminders to thaw meat the day before.
• Cook mode shows one step at a time, with timers.

KEEP THE FAMILY IN THE LOOP
• Text or email the menu and shopping list.
• Print a menu for the fridge.
• Little helpers: kid-sized kitchen jobs for every meal.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
• Works offline.
