Table Compass Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026 · Published by Waypoint Ink

Table Compass is a family meal planner that runs on your own device. We don't have accounts, servers or analytics, so we never see or collect your information.

What the app stores, and where

Your household name, recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, kitchen and pantry items, receipts, prices, garden notes, allergies, eating preferences, settings and any photos you add are saved only on your device, inside the app. None of it is sent to Waypoint Ink or anyone else. If you uninstall the app, this information is deleted from your device.

If you choose to save a backup or a PDF, the app saves a file to your device or opens your device's share sheet. What happens to that file afterward is up to you.

What we collect

Nothing. Table Compass does not collect, sell, share or track personal information. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Camera and photos

If you take or choose a photo of a recipe card, food label, receipt or a meal you cooked, the photo is processed on your device. Text in it is read on your device by a built-in reader. Photos are never uploaded.

Sending to family

When you choose to send a menu or shopping list by email or text, Table Compass opens your own email or messaging app with the message filled in. You choose who to send it to. Table Compass never sends anything itself.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders (for example, when to start cooking or take meat out of the freezer), they're scheduled on your device by your device's own notification system. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Table Compass is made for adults planning meals for their household. It is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
