Getting started

The first time you open Table Compass, it asks a few quick questions: how much you want to see (just meal planning, or all the extras), your household, busy nights, which meals to plan, and any allergies. Then it plans your first week. You can change any of it later in Settings.

Planning the week

Tap Re-plan week for a fresh plan, or tap the swap button on any meal to change just that one. Busy nights only get quick or slow-cooker meals, and the planner avoids repeating the same kind of dish in one week.

Adding your own recipes and photos

Open Recipes to add a recipe by typing it in or snapping a photo of a recipe card. To add a picture of a dish you made, open the recipe and tap Add your photo. It shows on that meal everywhere in the app.

Allergies

Set allergies once and Table Compass never plans a meal that contains them. Allergy detection reads ingredient names, so always check labels yourself.

Shopping list

Your list is built from the week's meals and sorted by store aisle. Check items off as you shop.

Printing and sending

Use Print for a fridge menu, recipes or shopping list. Use Send to family to email or text the menu and list.

Showing or hiding features

Go to Settings → Show or hide features to turn extras like the Kitchen tab, cook mode or prices on and off.

Moving to a new phone

Everything is saved only on your device. In Settings, save a backup, move the file to your new phone, and load it there.
