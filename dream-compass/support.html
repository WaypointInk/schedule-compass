Playing a sound

Tap any sound on the home screen: a signature mix, a solfeggio tone, a sleep beat, a water sound, singing bowls or dreamy music. It keeps playing when you lock your phone, and you can pause it from your Lock Screen.

Mixing your own

In the player, tap Mix to layer a tone, a sleep beat, music, water, singing bowls and far-off chimes, each with its own volume. Tap the heart to save a mix to Favorites.

Sleep timer

Tap the timer in the player to choose how long to play. The sound fades out gently at the end.

Headphones or speaker

Binaural beats need headphones. On the speaker, choose Speaker and the app uses gentle pulses instead.

Breathing guide

Open Breathe and pick a pattern. The light grows as you breathe in, rests while you hold, and shrinks as you breathe out.

Gentle wake-up and sunrise light

Set a time on the Wake tab and soft sound fades in instead of a loud alarm. Keep the app open and your phone charging. For the sunrise light, don't lock your phone: tap Good night — start sunrise light (or just leave the player open), and the screen stays dark until it glows warm orange at wake time. Tap Preview the sunrise light to see it.

If the sound stops

Tap Playback check at the top of the home screen and send us a screenshot.

A note on health

Dream Compass is for relaxation and sleep. It is not a medical treatment. Keep the volume low and comfortable.
