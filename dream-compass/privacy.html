Dream Compass Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026 · Published by Waypoint Ink

Dream Compass is a sleep and relaxation sound app that runs on your own device. We don't have accounts, servers or analytics, so we never see or collect your information.

What the app stores, and where

Your favorite mixes, volume levels, sleep timer, breathing and wake-up settings are saved only on your device, inside the app. None of it is sent to Waypoint Ink or anyone else. If you uninstall the app, this information is deleted from your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Dream Compass does not collect, sell, share or track personal information. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Sounds

Every tone, beat, water sound, chime, singing bowl and piece of music is made live on your device. Nothing is streamed or downloaded, and the app never uses your microphone.

Wake-up and reminders

If you set a gentle wake-up, it's scheduled on your device, and a backup notification is scheduled by your device's own notification system. The sunrise light only changes your screen's brightness while the app is open. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Dream Compass is made for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
