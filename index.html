      Google & Outlook calendars

      Bring your calendar in: export it as a calendar file (.ics) and load it here. Your events show up in your week and the planner works around them.

      How to get the file

      Google Calendar (on a computer): Settings → Import & export → Export. Unzip the download and pick your calendar's .ics file.

      Outlook.com: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → copy the ICS link, open it, and save the file. Outlook app: File → Save Calendar.

      Send your plan out: download this week's plan (classes, work, study, walks) and school dates as a file, then import it into Google Calendar (Settings → Import & export → Import) or Outlook (Add calendar → Upload from file).

      My family

      Add the people you plan with. Then pick who each plan is for on “Add plans.” Their plans show on your week in their color, and only the ones you're going to are planned around. Share one person's schedule, or everyone's, from Share.

      Chores

      Extras

      My school day

      Your planner builds every school day around these times, and plans homework time after school.

      My courses

      Credits set your weekly study time. Class times repeat every week; add the term's first and last day so they stop after the term. Online course with no set time? Leave the days blank.

      My routine

      Every day is built around these. On early-start days it wakes you earlier and moves bedtime up the night before, so you still get your sleep.

      My look

      Changes the whole planner, including printouts.

      Keep it safe

      Everything saves on this device and nothing is sent anywhere. Save a backup now and then so you can move your planner to a new computer.
