- **Description:**

Your week, planned around real life.

Schedule Compass is a weekly planner for high school and college students. Tell it your classes, credits, work shifts and sleep routine, and it plans every day for you, with study time, meals, rest and a little fun.

PLANS YOUR STUDY TIME
• Study time comes from your credits, about 2 hours a week per credit (you choose).
• It's spread across the days that have room, and moves when your schedule changes.

WORKS AROUND YOUR LIFE
• Add work shifts, appointments and plans, and every day rebuilds around them.
• Early shift? Your sleep and morning routine move too.
• A weekly walk or nature break is built in.

YOUR SCHOOL CALENDAR, BUILT IN
• Snap a photo of your school's academic calendar. Days off, registration deadlines and term dates show up in your week.
• Load a calendar file from Google Calendar or Outlook.

SHARE AND PRINT
• Text or email your day or week to friends and family.
• Print your week with writing lines or a notes page.

A KIND WORD EVERY DAY
• A new affirmation every day of the year.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
• Works offline.
