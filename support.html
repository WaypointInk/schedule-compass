Getting started

The first time you open Schedule Compass, it asks for your name, look, color, sleep routine and courses. After that it plans your week for you. Add work shifts under Add shifts & plans, where you can paste them straight from a work scheduling app.

How study time is set

Your study goal comes from your credits. It's 2 hours a week per credit by default, and you can change that under My courses, routine & look. Study time is spread across the days that have room, with a daily limit you control.

Reading my school calendar from a photo

Go to School dates & calendars and choose a photo or screenshot of your academic calendar. You'll check each date before it's added. The first time you do this you'll need an internet connection so the text reader can download. A clear, straight-on photo works best, and you can always paste the dates as text instead.

Google Calendar and Outlook

To bring in a calendar, export it as an .ics file and choose Load a calendar file. To send your plan out, choose Download this week (.ics) and import that file into Google Calendar or Outlook.

Moving to a new computer

Your planner is saved only on this device. Choose Save a backup, copy the file to your new computer, then choose Load a backup.

Printing

Open My week and use Print. You can print it horizontally or vertically, with writing lines, a full notes page, or your handwritten notes.
